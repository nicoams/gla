# Content

## Instantaneous foods

Display the available foods for all levels, their ingredients and their price.

## Forge

Display the:
- cost of boosting the set in crystals, in berries;
- the tries for each level of each piece of the set;
- the history of the crystals' prices;
- the history of crystals' transactions  (buy/sell on the black market)

## Boats

Display the materials needed to build the boats and their accessories per level.

## Characters

Display the characters by class/subclass and their requirements for achievements.
